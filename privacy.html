DJ Lights — Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026 · Applies to the DJ Lights app for Windows and its crowd web page

The short version

DJ Lights does not collect, store, sell or share your personal information. Sound is analysed live on the PC running the app to drive the lights. It is never recorded, saved or sent over the internet. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

What DJ Lights does

DJ Lights runs a light show on a PC and on phones connected to the same local network (Wi-Fi). The PC listens to music, and every connected screen shows lights that move in time with it.

Microphone and PC sound

  • When you choose This PC's sound or Microphone, the app processes audio in real time, on your PC only, to measure loudness in frequency bands and detect beats.
  • Audio is not recorded, stored or uploaded. Only small light-control values (for example "bass level 0.7, beat 112") are sent, and only over your local network to the screens taking part.
  • Capturing PC sound uses Windows screen-and-sound capture. The app discards the picture immediately and never shows, saves or sends it.
  • You can stop listening at any time with Stop, and you can turn off microphone access in Windows Settings → Privacy & security.

Local network

  • The app runs a small server on your PC so that phones on the same network can open the light show in their browser. This server is reachable only from your local network, and only while the app is open.
  • Phones that join receive light-control values, the selected pattern and colours, and any floating text the host types. That text is shown on connected screens and kept only in memory while the app runs.
  • The admin controls are protected by a PIN stored on your PC.

DJ Lights makes no connections to our servers or to any third party. The Microsoft Store may collect its own data, such as installs and crash reports, under Microsoft's Privacy Statement.

Children

DJ Lights does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes
